Installation Tips

Dual Channel Setup

Install modules in slots 2 and 4 (usually color-coded) for optimal dual-channel performance

Enable XMP/EXPO

Enter BIOS and enable XMP 3.0 (Intel) or EXPO (AMD) for rated speeds

Check Compatibility

Verify your motherboard's QVL (Qualified Vendor List) for memory compatibility

Proper Seating

Ensure modules click firmly into place and retention clips lock properly
