Intel Core™ i5-13600K Desktop Processor

Intel Core i5-13600K is a powerful gaming processor featuring 14 cores and 20 threads, with a max boost clock speed of up to 5.1GHz. Built on the Intel 7 process technology with enhanced performance cores and efficient cores.

  • 125W Processor Base Power
