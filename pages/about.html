Our Story

PCForge was founded by a group of passionate PC enthusiasts who saw the need for a user-friendly platform offering a wide selection of PC components. Over the years, we’ve grown into a trusted source for DIY PC builders around the world. We’re here to simplify the building process and offer the best deals on the most popular and reliable components available.
